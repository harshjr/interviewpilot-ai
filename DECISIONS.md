# DECISIONS.md

## 1. Why this approach over the obvious alternative I rejected?

I chose to build InterviewPilot AI as a product-first homepage instead of a typical SaaS landing page full of marketing claims and feature lists.

The obvious alternative was creating a standard homepage with sections explaining what the product does and why users should trust it. While that would have been quicker, I felt it wouldn't show the product in a meaningful way.

Instead, I focused on creating realistic product previews, including a mock interview experience, AI feedback panel, and readiness dashboard. This helps visitors immediately understand the value of the product without having to read through a lot of content. My goal was to make the homepage feel like a real product someone could start using today rather than just a concept being advertised.

---

## 2. One trade-off I made under the time limit, and what I would do with a real week.

Given the limited time, I decided to prioritize design quality, responsiveness, and the overall user experience instead of building every feature end-to-end.

The interview experience and analytics shown on the page are designed to demonstrate how the product would work, but they are not connected to a complete production backend. If I had a full week, I would build features such as user accounts, resume uploads, interview history, personalized feedback, and AI-powered interview sessions with persistent data storage.

The trade-off was choosing a polished and convincing product experience over a larger feature set, because I believed that best matched the goal of this assignment.

---

## 3. Where did I use AI tools, and what did I personally verify or change afterward?

I used AI tools to speed up brainstorming, explore different layout ideas, refine copy, and assist with implementation.

However, I did not use AI output without review. I manually checked the code, adjusted the page structure, rewrote sections of the content, improved spacing and visual hierarchy, tested responsiveness, and refined the overall experience to match the vision I had for the product.

AI helped me work faster, but the final decisions around design, user experience, content, and implementation were made and verified by me.
